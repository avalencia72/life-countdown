Add Life Counts

Install Life Counts before entering your dates so your personalized countdown is saved in the Home Screen app.

  1. Tap the Share button. If needed, open the Page Menu first and choose Share.
  2. Select Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App.
  4. Tap Add, then open Life Counts from your Home Screen and complete setup there.

The browser and Home Screen app keep separate private storage. Installing first prevents you from entering the same dates twice.

Settings

Personalize your horizons or privately transfer your setup between the browser and Home Screen app.

Life Counts has no account or cloud database. Export creates a private setup code that you control.

Transfer setup

The code contains your birthday, selected ages and optional milestone. It is encoded for transfer, not encrypted, so keep it private.

About this app

Purpose

The Life Counts App is a personal reflection and planning tool. It distinguishes an active-travel horizon, a lifetime planning horizon and a milestone worth anticipating. It is inspired by Four Thousand Weeks by Oliver Burkeman and Die With Zero by Bill Perkins. The app is not sponsored or endorsed by the authors or publishers.

Privacy

Your birthdate, selected ages, milestone name and milestone date are stored only in this browser on this device. The app has no account, database, cookies or analytics service, and the app itself does not transmit those entries to Albert Valencia. The hosting provider may process technical information under its own policies.

Terms and disclaimer

This app is for personal reflection and planning. It does not provide medical, actuarial, financial or legal advice. Population statistics are illustrative and are not predictions about any individual.
